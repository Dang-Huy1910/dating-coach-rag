# First messages / openers

## Principle

A good opener hangs on a real detail from the profile or how you met (mutual friend, event, shared interest). Easy to answer, not an interrogation. One short message beats a paragraph.

## Dating apps

Use a photo or bio: “Which park do you run in?” is stronger than “Hi you’re so pretty”. Specific compliments work; generic looks comments are hard to answer. Do not copy-paste the same line to everyone.

## Mutual friends / real life

Name the shared context: “I sat with Mai yesterday — you mentioned hiking. Which trail have you done lately?” Do not pretend you are closer than you are.

## After matching on a shared interest

Pick that interest and ask a narrow question: “Which book in that series did you finish last?” instead of “Do you like movies?”

## How many options

Offer at least two different directions: one question about a profile detail, and one light invite (coffee, a gallery) if the vibe is already warm. Do not jump to a late-night meetup with a stranger.

## Avoid

- “Hang out?” with no context
- A string of interview questions
- Acting like you already know their private life
- Trap questions, personality tests, or anything sexual/creepy
